You are the Creative Director, Lead Political Simulation Designer, Principal Gameplay Designer, UX Director, Narrative Designer, and Systems Architect for a AAA political strategy game.

Your task is to design the greatest political party creation experience ever made.

Do not simply create a "party creation screen."

Design an entire gameplay experience.

The result should feel so memorable that players remember founding their first political movement years later.

It should become one of the defining moments of the entire game.

=========================================================
PROJECT
=========================================================

Game: WORLDr

Genre:

Persistent multiplayer political simulation.

Players can become:

• Independent politician
• Party founder
• Party leader
• Opposition leader
• Coalition partner
• Prime Minister / President
• Small regional party
• National movement
• Governing coalition

The game is built around political influence rather than simply holding government.

Winning office is only one possible path.

=========================================================
MISSION
=========================================================

Design Party Creation as gameplay.

NOT as a form.

NOT as character customization.

NOT as a menu.

Players should feel they are founding an actual political movement that will shape history.

=========================================================
DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
=========================================================

Assume every existing political game failed to make party creation memorable.

Do not copy:

• Democracy 4
• The Political Process
• Suzerain
• Power & Revolution
• Lawgivers
• NationStates

Study their weaknesses.

Invent something better.

Think from first principles.

Challenge every assumption.

=========================================================
PRIMARY GOAL
=========================================================

The first two hours after founding a party should naturally teach players every major political system in the game.

Party Creation should become the tutorial without feeling like a tutorial.

=========================================================
PLAYER EMOTION
=========================================================

The player should experience:

Curiosity

Ownership

Responsibility

Excitement

Strategic planning

Political ambition

Risk

Identity

Long-term commitment

By the end, the player should genuinely feel:

"This is MY movement."

=========================================================
DESIGN FROM FIRST PRINCIPLES
=========================================================

Before designing anything, answer:

Why do political parties exist?

Why do people found parties?

Why do movements succeed?

Why do they fail?

What makes people join?

What creates loyalty?

What creates ideology?

What creates identity?

What creates momentum?

Everything must emerge from these answers.

=========================================================
PLAYER DECISIONS
=========================================================

Avoid fake choices.

Every decision should create meaningful trade-offs.

There should rarely be objectively correct answers.

Every strength should create weaknesses.

Every weakness should create opportunities.

=========================================================
SIMULATION
=========================================================

The world must react.

Media covers the launch.

Opposition attacks.

Citizens discuss it.

Businesses respond.

Interest groups evaluate it.

Polling changes.

Volunteers join.

Donations arrive.

Rumors spread.

Nothing should feel scripted.

=========================================================
MULTIPLAYER
=========================================================

Remember:

Many human parties exist simultaneously.

Design systems that remain balanced when hundreds or thousands of parties are competing.

Avoid mechanics that only work in single-player.

=========================================================
SCALABILITY
=========================================================

Assume players may spend 500–1000 hours with the same party.

The founding decisions should continue influencing gameplay decades later.

Avoid shallow mechanics that become irrelevant after the first hour.
