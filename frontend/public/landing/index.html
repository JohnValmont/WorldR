HOW IT WORKS

CHAPTER I

The Living World

Aethan does not pause when you log off. People, jobs and industries move every month; buyers choose cars by need and price; approval follows the economy people feel.

  • Drennia, Westmark and Eryndor: different governments, laws and economies, kept in balance.
  • Unemployment, growth and debt move approval, and approval moves elections.
  • Trade and treaties link the three nations.

CHAPTER II

The Political Rise

Join a party or found your own. Stand in a district, campaign, and win your seat. Every bill is a real vote, every coalition a written deal, and every minister holds a real brief.

  • District elections with seats shared out by vote.
  • Coalition talks with ministries and laws on the table.
  • Climb from citizen to head of government, or lose the leadership in a contest.

CHAPTER III

Business Power

Design a car, lease a factory, set your price and fight for buyers in every region. Computer-run rivals reprice and restock against you, and the laws Parliament passes change your costs.

  • A guided car designer with a live forecast from the real demand engine.
  • Rival car makers that react to your prices and your stock.
  • Bank loans, shareholders and dividends.

CHAPTER IV

Never an Empty World

Computer-run parties and companies fill every parliament and market, so the world works with ten players or a thousand. Real players are the bonus, not the requirement.

  • Computer-run parties table laws, vote by their beliefs and form governments.
  • Computer-run car makers compete for the same buyers you do.
  • A caretaker government keeps each nation running until one forms.
